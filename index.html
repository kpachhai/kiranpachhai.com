    • Protocol and standards

      Schedule Service tutorials and demos

      On most EVM chains a smart contract cannot wake up on its own: every call starts from an account or an off-chain bot, so time-based automation needs outside infrastructure. On Hedera, the Schedule Service lets a contract schedule future calls that the network itself executes.

      github.com/…/hedera-docs/blob/da7d0fdf921aca9cfb2545a956e37539706edad8/hedera/tutorials/smart-contracts/hss-x-evm-part-1-schedule-smart-contract-calls.mdx
